Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Prem Creation respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Prem Creation website and applications.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications are designed to provide their intended features without unnecessarily collecting personal information.

Depending on the application and features you use, the app may request access to device features such as storage, Wi-Fi, hotspot, network connections, camera, or location services. These permissions are used only when required for the application's features.

2. File Transfers

Applications that provide local file transfer functionality may access files that you explicitly select or choose to receive. Files are used for the requested transfer operation and are not intended to be collected by Prem Creation for unrelated purposes.

3. Network Connections

Some Prem Creation applications may use local Wi-Fi or hotspot connections to transfer information between compatible devices. The availability and behavior of these features depend on the Android version and device manufacturer.

4. Advertising

Some applications may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These services may use advertising identifiers and other information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Services

If an application uses third-party services, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies. Users should review the privacy policies of applicable third-party providers for additional information.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to Prem Creation.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to design our applications and services with privacy and security in mind. However, no internet or electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Prem Creation through our Contact page.
